# Indian Agricultural Crop Cultivation Guidelines

## Rice / Paddy Cultivation
- **Soil & Climate:** Alluvial or clay loam soils with high water holding capacity. Ideal temperature: 20°C - 35°C.
- **NPK Requirement:** 80-100 kg Nitrogen, 40-50 kg Phosphorus, 40-50 kg Potassium per hectare.
- **Water Requirement:** High (1200 - 1400 mm). Requires 5 cm standing water during tillering to panicle initiation stage.
- **Best Practices:** Use certified seeds (e.g. BPT 5204, MTU 1010). Apply 50% Urea as basal dose, 25% at tillering, and 25% at panicle stage.

## Cotton Cultivation
- **Soil & Climate:** Deep black soil (Regur) or fertile alluvial soil with good drainage. Warm climate (21°C - 30°C).
- **NPK Requirement:** 90-120 kg Nitrogen, 45-60 kg Phosphorus, 45-60 kg Potassium per hectare.
- **Water Requirement:** 600 - 800 mm. Sensitive to waterlogging.
- **Pest Advisory:** Monitor for pink bollworm and whitefly. Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) at early stage.

## Groundnut Cultivation
- **Soil & Climate:** Well-drained sandy loam or red loamy soil. Optimum temperature: 25°C - 30°C.
- **NPK Requirement:** 20-30 kg Nitrogen, 40-50 kg Phosphorus, 40-50 kg Potassium per hectare. (Legume fixes atmospheric N).
- **Gypsum Application:** Apply 400 kg Gypsum/ha at 45 days after sowing (pegging stage) for pod development and kernel filling.

## Chilli Cultivation
- **Soil & Climate:** Well-drained red or black loam soil rich in organic matter. Temperature: 20°C - 30°C.
- **NPK Requirement:** 120 kg Nitrogen, 60 kg Phosphorus, 60 kg Potassium per hectare.
- **Pest Advisory:** Protect against thrips and mites using yellow/blue sticky traps and recommended bio-pesticides.

## Maize Cultivation
- **Soil & Climate:** Deep fertile loamy or alluvial soil with pH 6.5 - 7.5. Warm weather (21°C - 27°C).
- **NPK Requirement:** 120-150 kg Nitrogen, 60 kg Phosphorus, 50 kg Potassium per hectare.
- **Management:** High nitrogen consumer; split application of Urea prevents leaching losses.
