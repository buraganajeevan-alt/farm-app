# Plant Protection, Pest & Disease Management Guide

## Integrated Pest Management (IPM)
- **Biological Control:** Use Neem oil (5 ml/L water + 1 ml liquid soap) or 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) as early preventive spray against sucking pests (aphids, thrips, whiteflies).
- **Physical Control:** Install 10-12 Yellow/Blue sticky traps per acre to capture flying pests. Install Pheromone traps for bollworm and stem borer monitoring.

## Common Fungal Diseases & Treatments
- **Blast Disease (Pyricularia oryzae in Paddy):** Spindle-shaped lesions on leaves with gray centers.
  - Treatment: Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L or Carbendazim @ 1 g/L. Avoid excess Nitrogen fertilizer.
- **Bacterial Leaf Blight (Xanthomonas):** Water-soaked yellow wilting streaks from leaf tips.
  - Treatment: Spray Copper Oxychloride @ 2.5 g/L mixed with Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L. Drain field water.
- **Tikka Leaf Spot (Groundnut):** Circular dark spots with yellow halo.
  - Treatment: Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2 g/L or Chlorothalonil @ 2 g/L.

## Organic Remedies
- **Jeevamrutha Preparation:** Blend 10 kg cow dung, 10 L cow urine, 2 kg jaggery, 2 kg pulse flour in 200 L water. Ferment for 48 hours. Fermented liquid enriches soil microflora and repels soil pathogens.
