# Indian Government Agricultural Schemes & Subsidies Guide

## PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Benefit:** Financial benefit of ₹6,000 per year provided in three equal installments of ₹2,000 every 4 months.
- **Eligibility:** All landholding farmer families with cultivable land in their names.
- **Registration:** Portal pmkisan.gov.in or nearest CSC center using Aadhaar, bank details, and land landholding documents.

## PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana - Crop Insurance)
- **Benefit:** Comprehensive crop insurance covering yield losses due to non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, pest attack, storm).
- **Premium Rates:** 
  - Kharif Crops: 2.0% of sum insured paid by farmer.
  - Rabi Crops: 1.5% of sum insured paid by farmer.
  - Commercial/Horticultural Crops: 5.0% of sum insured.

## Soil Health Card (SHC) Scheme
- **Benefit:** Provides soil nutrient status (12 parameters: N, P, K, pH, EC, Organic Carbon, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B) every 2 years along with crop-specific fertilizer recommendations.

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
- **Benefit:** Concessional short-term credit for crop cultivation at 4% interest rate (with prompt repayment incentive). Subsidized loan limit up to ₹3 Lakhs without collateral requirement up to ₹1.6 Lakhs.
