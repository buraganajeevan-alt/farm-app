# Fertilizer Management, NPK & Soil Health Guide

## NPK Principles
- **Nitrogen (N):** Promotes rapid vegetative growth, leaf color, and protein development. Sources: Urea (46% N), Ammonium Sulphate.
- **Phosphorus (P):** Essential for root establishment, flowering, and seed development. Sources: DAP (18-46-0), Single Super Phosphate (SSP - 16% P).
- **Potassium (K):** Enhances disease resistance, drought tolerance, and grain/fruit quality. Sources: Muriate of Potash (MOP - 60% K).

## Soil Testing & Health Card (SHC)
- **Soil pH:** Ideal range for most crops is 6.5 to 7.5.
  - Acidic Soil (< 6.0): Apply Agricultural Lime or Dolomite.
  - Alkaline Soil (> 8.0): Apply Gypsum or Elemental Sulphur with organic FYM (Farmyard Manure).
- **Micronutrient Deficiency:**
  - Zinc Deficiency (Kaira disease in paddy): Apply 25 kg Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4) per hectare.
  - Boron Deficiency: Apply 10 kg Borax/ha prior to sowing.

## Application Timings
- **Basal Dose:** 100% Phosphorus (DAP/SSP), 100% Potassium (MOP), and 30-50% Nitrogen applied during final land preparation.
- **Top-Dressing:** Remaining 50-70% Nitrogen split into 2-3 doses at active tillering/branching and flowering stages.
- **Rain Safety:** Never apply Urea top-dressing 24-48 hours before expected heavy rain to avoid nitrogen runoff into streams.
