Our marketplace allows you to procure certified seeds, balanced fertilizers (NPK, Urea), organic bio-pesticides, and Knapsack sprayers. Farmers can easily upload crop details, expected rates, and publish listings for bulk direct sales.

Resolve farm labor shortages by requesting callbacks from specialized local labor crews for tilling, transplanting, weeding, and harvesting. You can also broadcast custom job notifications detailing wage rates and start dates.
